Strategic Insight: Programmatic SEO

While the Wix website drives broad discovery, the Web App is currently earning its actual clicks through branded terms and specific portfolio projects (e.g., 'sauna sickness short film'). However, the app is also generating hundreds of un-clicked impressions for niche creator skills (e.g., 'copywriting luleå').

Recommendation: You have a massive opportunity in programmatic SEO. To turn those skill-based impressions into actual clicks, optimize the Web App's public profile metadata (Titles/Descriptions) to make them more enticing in Google's search results.

Behavioral Insight

More users prefer to start their journey using AI Search (17 users) rather than Manual Browse (9 users). However, users who browse manually tend to engage significantly deeper, triggering an average of 2.67 browse events compared to 1.29 searches.

Recommendation: Pay close attention to the scroll drop-off between 50% and 75%. Almost half (44%) of your engaged scrollers abandon the page there. Review the content in that specific middle section of the front page—it might need stronger calls-to-action or better visual hooks to keep them moving down to the 90% mark.
