Strategic Insight: Programmatic SEO

Your ecosystem is serving two distinct search intents. The Wix website captures broad marketing queries (e.g., "kommunikationsplan"), acting as the primary discovery engine. Meanwhile, Google is indexing the Web App for highly specific creator names and geo-located skills (e.g., "copywriting luleå").

Recommendation: Embrace this dual strategy. Optimize the Wix site's blog/landing pages for broad Swedish marketing terms to drive up that 78 click count. For the Web App, ensure public user profiles have clean metadata (Titles/Descriptions) to capitalize on the long-tail niche searches, turning your user base into a programmatic traffic engine.

Behavioral Insight

More users prefer to start their journey using AI Search (13 users) rather than Manual Browse (8 users). However, users who browse manually tend to engage significantly deeper, triggering an average of 2.50 browse events compared to 1.38 searches.

Recommendation: Pay close attention to the scroll drop-off between 50% and 75%. Half of your engaged scrollers abandon the page there. Review the content in that specific middle section of the front page—it might need stronger calls-to-action or better visual hooks to keep them moving down to the 90% mark.
