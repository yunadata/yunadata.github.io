My First Internship Day at MetaGruppen

Published on 2026-03-02 | Internship • Digital Marketing • SEO

🚀 Today I kicked off my first student internship (LIA 1) at MetaGruppen, and what an educational first day it has been! 🚀

Right from the start, I got to dive straight into the business and see how they work in practice, thanks to my supervisor Patric Melander! Here is a little bit of what my day consisted of:

  • Tech & Tools: The day started with a thorough walkthrough of the company's own SEO tool, MetaRanking. It was super exciting to get an insight into how they strategically analyze and work with search engine optimization.
  • Workflows & Meetings: I got to join a team meeting where both completed and upcoming tasks were reviewed. We also discussed the transition to their new internal communication system. Very fun to immediately get a feel for the company culture and their processes!
  • Practical SEO Work: Of course, I also got to pitch in right away! My first task was to go through a client's blog posts and strategically build up relevant internal links to strengthen the website's SEO.

A big thank you to the whole team at MetaGruppen for a warm welcome. I am very much looking forward to continuing to develop here over the coming weeks! 🌟
