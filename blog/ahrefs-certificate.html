Certified in Ahrefs

Published on 2025-12-17 | Certificate • SEO & Performance • Digital Marketing

I’m thrilled to share a new milestone in my learning journey: I am officially certified in the Ahrefs’ Marketing Platform!

Why this matters for my studies: This certificate gave me a deep dive into the “how.” It wasn’t just about checking boxes; it was about learning how to dissect competitor data, audit site health, and turn raw SEO metrics into actionable insights.

My biggest takeaways:

  • Data-Driven SEO: Moving beyond basic keywords to understand the metrics behind search intent.
  • Competitive Intelligence: Learning how to reverse-engineer successful strategies using real-world data.
  • Technical Auditing: Spotting the technical issues that can make or break a site’s performance.

A huge thank you to Sam Oh and Andrei Țiț for the incredible instruction. The way you broke down complex analytics concepts made them easy to grasp and apply.

I’m excited to add this certificate to my resume and apply these new skills to my future projects!
